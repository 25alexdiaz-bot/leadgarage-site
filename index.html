Straight answers.

What kinds of businesses is LeadGarage made for?

Field-service businesses such as pest control, lawn care, and exterior-service companies that need customer follow-up, scheduling, and billing in one place.

How does automatic lead status work?

A scheduled service sets Service Scheduled. Cancelling or deleting that service returns the lead to Contact Made. Paid work moves the customer to Paid Customer, while overdue unpaid work becomes Past Due.

Can LeadGarage handle recurring service?

Yes. Create monthly recurring service for 1, 3, 6, 9, or 12 months, or choose a custom end date. Every visit is a separate service record with its own cost and payment state.

How are notes organized?

Customer Notes are editable internal-only remarks pinned to the record. Follow-Up Notes form a dated timeline for calls, results, and ongoing context.

What happens when a customer is archived?

The record is removed from active work without being deleted. It can be restored later with its full customer, service, payment, and note history intact.

Is company data separated?

Yes. LeadGarage uses real user logins and keeps each company’s records separated. Users can also change their password or log out from the side navigation.
